# Daymark design system

Daymark is designed as a quiet, encouraging workspace: a warm paper-like canvas, deep evergreen structure, and a restrained terracotta accent. The interface favors legible hierarchy and clear next steps over decorative gradients and busy motion.

## Visual language

- **Canvas:** warm off-white (`#f6f5f0`), with a matching soft-dark mode.
- **Primary structure:** evergreen (`#30443b`) for the daily focus panel and muted sage for supporting states.
- **Action accent:** terracotta (`#dd7254`) for primary actions, active navigation, and focus details.
- **Typography:** Manrope for display and section headings; DM Sans for controls and reading.
- **Surfaces:** quiet solid cards, fine borders, modest shadows, and rounded corners.

## Experience

- Today opens on a selected focus task, daily completion progress, and fast task filters.
- Calendar keeps scheduled work visible in a compact monthly view with selected-day details.
- Notes are searchable and filterable by tags, with straightforward export.
- Insights summarize completion, current streak, tasks due today, and a seven-day trend.
- Navigation adapts to a compact bottom bar on small screens.
- Keyboard shortcuts: `Ctrl/Cmd + K` or `/` opens search; `Ctrl/Cmd + N` starts a task; `Esc` clears search.

The layout respects reduced-motion preferences, uses visible keyboard focus, and labels icon-only controls for assistive technology.
